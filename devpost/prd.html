⚙️ Features and Behavior

⏳ Era Roster

Pattern: fast · standard · heavy enemies. Cheap trap counters the fast enemy, mid trap the standard enemy, expensive trap the heavy enemy. Counter traps hit everyone, but hit their target harder.

RoleEnemyCounter trapUses

🚦 States and Boundaries

First play
Era 1, Wave 1, empty Inventory, some starting money ASSUMPTION, full castle health.
Empty Inventory, no money
The picker shows the 3 traps dimmed and tells the player to tap the castle to earn money.
Occupied cell
One trap per cell; tapping it shows remaining uses ASSUMPTION.
Not enough money/resources
Buy and Upgrade buttons are disabled and show what is missing.
Final era
In Era 5 the Upgrade button shows "Max era".
Persistence
Nothing is saved; closing the tab ends the run.

📦 Building · Deferred · Non-Goals

✅ What We're Building
  • Main Menu (logo, background, Play, How to Play)
  • Game Screen: HUD, grid map with 4 two-cell-wide paths, central castle
  • Click castle for money · Shop + Inventory · trap picker on path cells
  • Traps with limited uses and counter bonuses
  • 5 eras × 3 enemies × 3 traps · 20 waves/era with Wave-20 repeat · resource drops · castle damage
  • Castle Upgrade with health restore and full era swap
  • Speed button · Win/Defeat Stats Screen
⏸️ Deferred From the POC & Later Enhancements
  • Upgrading individual traps within an era
  • Detailed hand-drawn Kingdom Rush–quality art
  • Saving progress
  • Later: multiple maps & difficulty, sound & music, pause/settings
🚫 Non-Goals
  • Accounts or login
  • Installing anything
  • Selling traps
  • Copying Kingdom Rush / Age of War gameplay

❓ Open Questions

2. Balance numbers — money per click, prices, uses (assumed 1/3/5), damage, health, drops, upgrade costs, old-trap refund (assumed price × remaining uses ÷ total uses). Tune during build.
